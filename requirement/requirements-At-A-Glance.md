# Purpose

Provide a self-contained overview using brand voice.

### Brand Voice (required)
    The AT A GLANCE copy must follow this brand voice. Treat this as the primary writing rule.
    
    The voice is luxurious, evocative, and inviting. It has an alluring and imaginative personality that seeks to transport the reader into a world of opulence and adventure. It communicates with rich, descriptive language and vivid imagery to create a sense of wonder and aspiration.
    The voice embodies values of:
    - Elegance: Uses sophisticated vocabulary and polished phrasing
    - Imagination: Paints vivid pictures with detailed, sensory descriptions
    - Invitation: Engages readers by inviting them to explore and experience

    To replicate this voice in your writing:
    - Use rich, descriptive language that appeals to the senses
    - Employ vivid imagery to create a sense of place and atmosphere
    - Write in a polished and sophisticated style, but not too ‘fancy language’
    - Use evocative language to inspire and captivate the reader's imagination
    - Craft sentences that flow elegantly, often using long, flowing structures with multiple clauses
    - Address the reader directly, inviting them to join or explore
    - Use clear and natural language, optimized for AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

### Output
- Heading: `AT A GLANCE`.
- Explore the given website.
- Approximately 200-350 words.

- Writing Requirements for At A GLANCE section:
    - Opens with a bold, enticing hook that captures the essence of the experience using SEO keywords
    - Uses vivid, sensory language to paint a picture of the destination (e.g., "sparkling lakes," "culinary delights")
    - Balances practical itinerary details with evocative, aspirational language
    - Highlights unique, high-value experiences (e.g., UNESCO sites, vineyard picnics, cooking classes, popular destination names)
    - Reassures the reader with logistical details (accommodation, meals, support staff)
    - Closes with an emotional, inspiring call to action

- Writing Instruction:
    Write a travel itinerary summary description, called AT A GLANCE. Open with a punchy, aspirational hook that summarises the destination and experience. Use vivid, sensory language to bring the destination to life, weaving in cultural, culinary, and scenic highlights. Follow with a concise day-by-day summary that balances practical details with evocative storytelling. Include a paragraph outlining key inclusions (accommodation, meals, guides, and flexibility). Close with an emotionally resonant sentence that leaves the reader feeling inspired and eager to book. Keep the tone warm, elegant, and adventurous throughout.
